# CODE OF CONDUCT

## Our Pledge

We are committed to making participation in the DAB Control Suite project a welcoming, respectful and constructive experience for everyone.

We value:

- Respect
- Curiosity
- Collaboration
- Transparency
- Constructive feedback
- Professional behaviour

---

## Expected Behaviour

Contributors are encouraged to:

- Be respectful and patient.
- Assume good intentions.
- Focus discussions on technical topics.
- Provide constructive criticism.
- Help newcomers.
- Share knowledge openly.
- Respect different levels of experience.

---

## Unacceptable Behaviour

The following is not acceptable:

- Harassment or discrimination
- Personal attacks
- Offensive language
- Deliberate disruption of discussions
- Publishing private information
- Spam or malicious contributions

---

## Engineering Principles

The DAB Control Suite follows these development principles:

- Analyse before modifying.
- Verify instead of guessing.
- Keep changes small and focused.
- Document important decisions.
- Test before declaring work complete.
- Prefer maintainable solutions over quick fixes.

---

## Reporting Problems

If you experience unacceptable behaviour, please contact the project maintainer.

Project Founder & Lead Developer

---

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove comments, reject contributions or restrict participation if this Code of Conduct is violated.

Our goal is always to resolve problems fairly, respectfully and transparently.

Thank you for helping build a friendly and professional open-source community around the DAB Control Suite.
